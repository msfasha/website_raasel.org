Acceptable Use Policy — Raasel

Last updated: February 22, 2026

Raasel is a private, encrypted messaging platform.

Users must not use Raasel to:

  • Distribute illegal content of any kind
  • Harass, bully, or threaten individuals
  • Spread misinformation or disinformation
  • Send unsolicited bulk messages (spam)
  • Attempt to hack or disrupt the service
  • Impersonate others

Reporting violations: abuse@raasel.com

Violations may result in immediate account termination.
