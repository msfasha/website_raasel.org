Privacy Policy — Raasel Light

Last updated: August 5, 2026  ·  Applies to the Android app com.raasel.light

The short version. Raasel Light is a client for the open Matrix protocol. It has no server of its own. Your account and your messages live on whichever Matrix homeserver you choose when you sign in — we never receive, store, or relay them. The only data that reaches Raseen Labs is anonymous crash diagnostics, and only when the app crashes.

1. Who we are

Raasel Light is published by Raseen Labs. This policy covers the Raasel Light Android application only. It does not cover the separate Raasel service at raasel.com, which has its own policy, and it does not cover the Matrix homeserver you connect to.

Privacy contact: info@raseenlabs.com

2. How the app works, and why that matters here

Raasel Light is a "bring your own homeserver" client. At sign-in you enter a Matrix user ID and password, and the app talks directly to that homeserver over HTTPS. Raseen Labs operates no intermediary. In practical terms:

  • Your credentials are sent to your homeserver, not to us.
  • Your messages, photos, and voice notes are sent to your homeserver, not to us.
  • Your contacts and room memberships are held by your homeserver, not by us.

The privacy practices of your chosen homeserver — retention, logging, IP address handling, lawful-access policy — are set by whoever operates it, and are outside our control. If you are unsure who runs your homeserver, ask before signing in.

4. Data stored on your device

Raasel Light keeps a local SQLite database on your phone containing your session token, your end-to-end encryption keys, and a cache of recent messages. This is what allows the app to stay logged in and to decrypt past conversations. It is stored in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device.

Signing out, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app removes this local database. Note that removing your encryption keys without a saved recovery key may make previously received encrypted messages permanently unreadable on that device.

5. Third-party services

Sentry (crash reporting)

Raasel Light uses Sentry to report crashes. When the app crashes, Sentry may receive a stack trace, the app version, the operating system version, the device model, and a breadcrumb trail of in-app navigation events leading up to the failure. This is used solely to diagnose defects.

Sentry is not present in the messaging code path, and message content, contact lists, photos, voice recordings, and encryption keys are not sent to it.

Nothing else

Raasel Light contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no tracking pixels, and no attribution or fingerprinting libraries. We do not sell or rent data to anyone, and we run no profiling or behavioural advertising of any kind.

7. Encryption

All communication with your homeserver uses HTTPS. In rooms where end-to-end encryption is enabled, message content is additionally encrypted on your device using vodozemac, the Matrix project's own audited cryptographic library, so that only participating devices can read it. Neither Raseen Labs nor your homeserver operator can read the contents of encrypted messages.

8. Children

Raasel Light is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help you address it — though note that any account data would sit with the relevant homeserver, not with us.

9. Your choices and deletion

Because the app holds no server-side account of its own, deletion works in two parts:

  • Data on your device — sign out from Settings, or uninstall the app, or clear its data from Android Settings. This is immediate and complete.
  • Your Matrix account and message history — these belong to your homeserver. To delete or export them, use that homeserver's own account-deletion process, or contact its administrator. We have no ability to delete data from a server we do not run.
  • Crash reports — to request deletion of crash diagnostics associated with your device, email info@raseenlabs.com and we will remove them from Sentry.
