Privacy Policy — Raasel

Last updated: February 22, 2026

1. Who we are

Raasel ("we", "us") operates the Raasel messaging app available at raasel.org. Contact: privacy@raasel.org

2. What we collect

  • Account registration data (username, optional email/phone)
  • Device identifiers for push notifications
  • IP address (used by our messaging service for connection)
  • App usage metadata (if analytics is enabled)

3. What we do NOT collect

  • Message content (all messages are end-to-end encrypted)
  • We cannot read your messages

4. How we use your data

  • To provide and maintain the messaging service
  • To send push notifications
  • To investigate abuse reports

5. Data sharing

  • We do not sell your data
  • Data may be shared with service operators as required to deliver the service
  • We may disclose data if required by law

6. Data retention

  • Account data is retained until you delete your account
  • You can request deletion by contacting privacy@raasel.org

7. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Contact us at privacy@raasel.org

8. Security

  • All messages are end-to-end encrypted
  • We use industry-standard security practices

9. Changes

We will notify users of significant changes via the app.
